<TeacherNote title="Checking a robot against this file">

Most of Module 4 runs with the robot sitting still. These tests only print, so
you can check a student's planner from their file without a grid:
`test_paper_paths()`, `test_edge_cases()`,
`test_planner()` (six lines, all `PASS`) and `test_headings()`. The ones that drive
need the grid: `test_drive_path()` and the graded `test_delivery()`, which students
build in Lesson 4-7 one leg at a time before the loop.

`turn_to()` turns clockwise one quarter at a time until the heading is right, as
the lesson has it, so a left turn comes out as three right turns. That is the
lesson's design, not a bug.

</TeacherNote>

</TeacherNote>
